WHALE-STATE LENS - PRE-DECLARED GRID (written before any TEST-period (22.09-29.09) outcome was computed or looked at)
Written: 2026-09-30 ~21:05 UTC, after discovery exploration (explore_disc.py, regress_state.py disc) only.

UNIVERSE U (events_state.parquet / build_events.py):
  whale close tx (liq==0, Binance symbol) at which the wallet's rolling-60s close in this coin & direction reaches >=5% of the
  position (sz / |startPosition| at the start of the 60 s) AND >=$5k, position ratio (batch_ratio) >=1.
  Episode dedupe: per addr x coin x whale-trade-direction, a new event only after >30 min without such a tx.
  Decision time dec = ceil(max(tx_ts + 1.5 s, bot_ts)) (bot_ts = when the bot actually saw the tx).
  Periods: DISCOVERY = events with UTC day <= 2026-09-21 ; TEST = 2026-09-22 .. 2026-09-29.

STATES (all computed from information known at dec; other wallets' txs count only if their bot_ts <= dec):
  HERD30_1    exactly 1 other tracked wallet closed the same coin in the same direction (>= $1k) in the 30 min before the tx
  HERD30_2P   >= 2 such other wallets in 30 min
  HERD120_3P  >= 3 such other wallets in 120 min
  HERDUSD30   other wallets' same-direction close USD in 30 min > 0.5 x HL 1%-depth
  OPP30       other wallets' OPPOSITE-direction close USD in 30 min > 0.1 x depth (disagreement)
  FRESH6      first material close after >= 6 h without any >= $1k close of this wallet in this coin (only if wallet tracked >= 6 h)
  NEARFULL    rolling-60s close >= 50% of the position
  MULTICOIN   wallet closed >= 3 distinct coins (>= $1k txs) before the event (past-only)
  WDISTRESS   whale PnL at the close < -3% (price return from whale entry; entry from HL API closedPnl; events without API data excluded)
  WPROFIT     whale PnL at the close > +3%

HORIZONS / METRICS (our side = whale trade direction for 'fol', opposite for 'fad'):
  S300, S1800 : entry worst-in-3s on Binance 1s bars at [dec, dec+2] (buy=max high, sell=min low; fallback 10 s),
                exit worst-in-3s at dec+H. net = gross - 0.146 - side * EW-alt-index move over the window (market-neutral).
  L8, L24     : entry close of the 1m candle opening at the first full minute after dec, exit h hours later;
                net = side*(coin - EW-alt index) - 0.146 - funding paid by our side (HL hourly funding proxy). Hedge-leg cost NOT included
                (sensitivity +0.10 reported).
  Dedupe per cell: one open trade per coin & our side; an event is skipped while a previous kept trade of the same coin & side is open.

GRID: 10 states x 4 horizons = 40 cells. Direction of each cell = the better of fol/fad by DISCOVERY net mean (so K on discovery = 80).
Discovery candidate criteria: n >= 15, net mean > 0, net median > 0, state-label null p < 0.10.
Null: shuffle the state label within coin x day among events whose state is known (500 perms), recompute cell (with dedupe) mean;
  p = P(null mean >= observed). Also count cells with p < 0.05 vs 40*0.05 = 2 expected by chance.
TEST: evaluate ALL 40 cells once in the declared direction; a candidate survives only if test mean > 0, median > 0,
  CI90 of the mean (cluster bootstrap by coin AND by day) above 0, both halves > 0, sum without top-10% > 0, and
  no coin / wallet / day > 50% of the positive sum.
